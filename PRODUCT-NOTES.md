# Prompt Pocket Product Notes

## Beth live feedback automation — concept, not shipped

The proposed automation gives Beth a direct, real-time, two-way chat and feedback channel. It must be possible to activate and deactivate the automation deliberately rather than leaving it permanently active.

Before this becomes a home-screen automation card, define:

- which chat or channel Beth uses;
- who may activate or deactivate it;
- what “active” permits the automation to send, receive, or retain;
- whether either participant must approve messages before delivery;
- what conversation history is stored, for how long, and who can delete it;
- what should happen when the automation is inactive;
- the visible status and emergency stop behavior.

Do not represent this as available until the two-way delivery path, permission boundary, off switch, and read-back verification have been tested end to end.
